How the real 435 seats of the U.S. House are divided among the states by real population -- a real math system, not an opinion. Every number here is real historical Census Bureau data.
The Real Formula
priority = population ÷ √(n × (n−1))
Each state gets 1 guaranteed seat. For every seat after that, this real formula scores how strong a state's claim is to "seat number n." The two highest scores nationwide get the next two seats, and so on, until all 435 are handed out.
