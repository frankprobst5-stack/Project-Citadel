# Learning Path: 3rd Grade → Graduation

Started 2026-09-28, for Frank's own son (see the memory-tracked profile in
`[[project_cloud9_target_learner]]` -- real, not a hypothetical student).
Real anchor requirement, confirmed live against OPM's own Meteorology
Series 1340 standard: a career with the NWS needs a bachelor's with 24
semester hours in atmospheric science, 6 hours of physics with lab, and
names **calculus as a real prerequisite/corequisite** for the atmospheric
dynamics, physics, and differential equations coursework. Engineering
programs want the same real math/physics core plus chemistry. Everything
below works backward from that.

Two real eras, per `ROADMAP.md`'s own locked "long horizon" decision:

- **Grades 3-6: Cloud9-proper.** Detailed, year by year, subject by
  subject -- this is what Cloud9 delivers directly, soon, and it's worth
  being concrete now.
- **Grades 7-12: the successor project's era** (placeholder name "Cloud9
  Next"). Real milestones only -- what has to be true by when -- not
  product design. That's real, separate work, better done with years of
  running Cloud9 behind it.

## What this document is not

Not a claim that Cloud9-the-app will someday teach Calculus. The honest
plan, per `ROADMAP.md`'s own locked decision: Cloud9 delivers grades 3-6
for real, directly. The successor project delivers the rest, likely
orchestrating real outside resources (Kolibri's own imported course
library if it reaches that depth, dual enrollment, AP self-study) rather
than Cloud9 rebuilding a full high school from scratch — the same
"orchestrate, don't rebuild" principle this whole project has used from
Kolibri to GeoGebra to NOAA's own data, just applied at the scale of a
whole education instead of one Lab.
