    START Triage (Multiple-Casualty Sorting)

    Standard Simple Triage And Rapid Treatment algorithm — use only when there are more patients than you can treat individually and you must decide treatment order.
    1. Can they walk? Direct all walking wounded to one area. Tag GREEN — Minor.
    2. Are they breathing? If not, open the airway. Still not breathing → tag BLACK — Deceased/Expectant. If breathing starts after repositioning → tag RED — Immediate.
    3. Breathing rate. Over 30 breaths/min → tag RED — Immediate. 30 or under, continue.
    4. Circulation. Check radial pulse or capillary refill. No radial pulse / refill over 2 seconds → tag RED — Immediate.
    5. Mental status. Can't follow simple commands → tag RED — Immediate. Can follow commands → tag YELLOW — Delayed.

      Medication Storage Guidance

      • Keep in original labeled containers — never mix medications into unlabeled bags or bottles.
      • Store away from heat, direct sun, and humidity; check each label's specific storage temperature.
      • Track expiration dates in Supplies & Inventory and rotate stock.
      • This page intentionally does not calculate doses. Always follow the medication's own label instructions, a pharmacist, or a trained medical provider.

          Infection Control

          • Wash hands or use hand sanitizer before and after treating any wound.
          • Wear gloves whenever available, especially with any blood or body fluid contact.
          • Clean wounds with clean water; avoid re-touching a cleaned wound with unwashed hands.
          • Dispose of used dressings and sharps safely, away from where people live.

          Poison Control Information

          US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222 (free, confidential, available anytime service is reachable). Verify and record the correct number for your own region here if different.
