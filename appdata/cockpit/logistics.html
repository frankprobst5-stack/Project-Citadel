🎯 Crisis-Readiness Score

The water target below is still a real BBK-standard calculation (2L per person per day) with no unit data to reconcile it against. The food energy target now does reconcile against real stock, Phase 7 #3's own self-sufficiency calculator, below — but only using items that actually have a kcal/unit entered on their own ledger row (Food & Freezer tabs); anything without one is honestly excluded from the total rather than counted as zero calories. The score itself is computed from what Citadel genuinely does track: whether water/food/freezer have anything logged at all, how many tracked items are at or below their own low-stock threshold, and how many have a real, unexpired date on file.
