📚 PROJECT CITADEL // OFFLINE KNOWLEDGE MATRIX

New install? This library ships empty on purpose. Citadel doesn't bundle any offline content itself — real Kiwix archives (Wikipedia, Wiktionary, medical/survival references, etc.) are often several GB each, and choosing what's worth the space is your own call. Download real .zim files from library.kiwix.org, drop them into appdata/kiwix-library/, then restart the Kiwix container (docker compose restart kiwix) or just re-open this page — new books show up automatically, no re-install needed.
