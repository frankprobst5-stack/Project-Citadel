Backups

Backs up everything under appdata/ plus .env, except large/regenerable content (downloaded AI models, the Kolibri course library, your media vault's own video/audio/PDF library). Backup files contain real secrets (API tokens, your exact station location if set) — store them somewhere secure, not casually shared.

Restore From Upload

Restoring overwrites your current live data with whatever's in the backup. A safety snapshot of your current state is always taken automatically first, so this is never a one-way door — but treat it as a real, deliberate action, not a casual click. After any restore, run docker compose restart for the whole fleet (no service name) — other containers that share a restored directory (e.g. the cockpit dashboard) can otherwise show a stale/empty view until restarted, even though the underlying data restored correctly.
